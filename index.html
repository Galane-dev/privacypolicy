04

Grade 12 All Subjects

Learning, practice and Matric+

The app processes chosen subjects and chapters, quiz answers, attempts, scores, study time, streaks, goals, timetable entries and progress. Exam-readiness features use practice activity to produce topic summaries, estimated readiness and study suggestions. Signed-in readiness records can be saved in Firestore and sent to the AWS exam-readiness service where that service is enabled.

Matric+ and Course Finder: signed-in learners' subject marks are saved in Firestore for use across devices, with local copies for offline use. Career-assessment answers, funding details, saved opportunities and application-planner entries are currently kept on the device. Funding answers may include financial circumstances or disability information; provide only what is needed for the match you want.

Mock-exam answers, paused attempts and attempt history are currently saved locally. Where AI marking is offered, the typed answer and relevant question and marking context are submitted for assessment. Scores used by the readiness feature may also form part of cloud-saved practice records.

Personal notes, downloads and audio

Private highlights, drawings, edits, added notes and edit-history timestamps are stored locally until you publish or share a version. Publishing uploads the selected customisation and its title, description, subject, chapter and author details. The publish-and-share workflow also creates a social post linking to your notes. Review your edits and remove private information before publishing. Pro or rewarded-ad access controls access to the shared version; it does not prevent an authorised reader from copying or sharing what they see.

Downloaded papers and audio lessons, playback positions and visual-audio caches are stored on the device. Playing a pre-generated lesson retrieves an existing recording; it does not record your voice or generate a personal voice model. Online download requests still reach the storage provider.

Community, schools, tutors and study partners

Optional social features process profiles, photos, school and subject details, posts, stories, comments, replies, reactions, poll or quiz responses, follows, bookmarks, mentions, messages, attachments and moderation reports. Tutor and study-partner features may include subjects, prices, availability, approximate area and contact information that you choose to provide. Contact details submitted for a public listing can become public.

Referral and shared-note features use account or installation identifiers, invitation links, access events and reward records to recognise participation, calculate rewards and limit duplicate claims. Guest access can use an installation identifier even where no signed-in account is required.

05

Simple Study and your PDFs

  1. You choose a PDF and page range. The current workspace reads and previews the selected file in your browser. It does not upload the original PDF to our Firebase Storage as part of the study-pack workflow.
  2. You ask for a study pack. Text extracted from the selected pages, page count, module name, study format and optional learning goal are sent to our server and then to OpenAI to generate your pack. Personal information contained in that text is included in the request.
  3. The result is saved to your account. Firestore stores the generated notes, recall cards, quiz, takeaways and revision plan, together with your account ID, original filename, selected page range, module, format, goal, title and creation time. Your library references and remaining credits are also updated.
  4. You study or export it. Saved packs can be reopened from your account. An exported PDF is stored wherever you choose to save it. Sharing that file gives its recipients a copy outside our control.

The pack-generation request does not deliberately include your email address as a prompt field, but names, student numbers or other identifiers inside your source text, module name or goal will be included if you supply them. Remove unnecessary personal information first.

Material you submit: only provide documents and information you are entitled to use and send for the processing described here. Remove unnecessary names, student numbers and third-party details. Do not submit confidential university, employer, patient or research-participant records without the necessary authority and lawful basis. These requirements apply to free and paid use.

Simple Study processes the selected material to produce and save the study pack you request. Uploading it does not give us ownership of the source or permission to publish it as public content. Saved packs are not automatically posted to the Grade 12 social feed.

Simple Study has separate credits and a Yoco checkout. To delete an account or saved pack, use the email privacy request route.

06

AI processing and recommendations

AI features require sending relevant material to a provider. The material depends on what you use:

  • Study chat and paper help: your question, selected chapter notes or paper, attachments you submit, and conversation context. Some chat histories and attachments are saved for later use.
  • Community AI tutors: a help request or mention, the relevant post and comment thread, subject or chapter context, and information included in that conversation. These tutors are automated accounts, not human teachers. Their responses in a public thread are public.
  • Flashcards, explanations and marking: relevant learning material, question and marking guidance, your answer or a topic-performance summary.
  • Simple Study: the selected-page text and study-pack inputs described above.

The current services use OpenAI and, for some Grade 12 tools, Google Gemini. Providers receive the submitted content and technical information needed to process a request. Availability and processing can vary by tool. A material provider or purpose change requires an updated notice and any legally required choice before the new use.

OpenAI's API terms provide that submitted data is not used for model training by default unless the API customer opts in. Requests and responses may be retained for abuse monitoring or the operation of the selected API feature. See OpenAI's API data controls for the applicable retention settings and exceptions.

Google's Gemini data-use rules depend on the provider service plan. Its unpaid-service terms permit product improvement and human review of submitted content and responses; its paid-service terms do not use that content for product improvement but allow limited safety-related retention. These refer to our provider arrangement, not whether you have Pro or buy credits. See Gemini's terms. Do not submit sensitive personal information to these tools. Contact us for confirmation about a feature's processing arrangement before submitting private material.

How to use AI results

Generated lessons, explanations, marking feedback and recommendations are educational support. They can contain errors or omissions and do not guarantee an exam result, admission or funding. Readiness and matching features use the answers, marks and available criteria supplied to them; their estimates are not official assessment or admissions decisions.

Check important answers against your course material and confirm entry requirements and closing dates with the relevant university or funder. You can correct your input and contact us about inaccurate personal information or a disputed result. AI responses are not a substitute for professional medical, legal or financial advice.

Avoid sending passwords, identity numbers, bank details, private contact information or sensitive third-party data in a prompt. To stop new submissions, stop using the AI tool; you can separately request removal of saved conversations. Permission to use one AI feature is not permission to publish your content or enrol you in marketing.

07

Public content and private conversations

Display names, handles, profile images, public profile fields, published posts, comments, shared-note descriptions and public achievements may be visible to other learners or anyone able to open the relevant shared content. Leaderboards may display your name or handle, image, score or study-time information. Visibility depends on the feature, not simply on whether you have Pro.

Messages and school chats are intended for their participants and authorised service operation or safety review. They are not end-to-end encrypted. Recipients can copy, forward or screenshot messages. Moderation reports may include the reported content and relevant surrounding context for investigation.

When you post, tag a tutor, upload an attachment or publish notes, consider everyone who could receive it. Do not publish someone else’s personal information without authority. Removing an item from our service cannot erase copies already saved by recipients or search engines; we will still consider requests to remove information we control.

Story expiry controls how long a story appears in the viewer. Related media, reports and recipient copies may remain after it stops appearing; our storage practices and deletion route are explained in retention and your choices.

Advertising, analytics and storage

Ads in Grade 12 All Subjects

AdMob supplies ads such as native, banner, rewarded and app-open placements. Google and any enabled mediation providers may process advertising or installation identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device and app details, ad impressions, interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition and detect fraud. Third-party ad technology may use cookies, local storage, SDK identifiers or similar technologies.

iOS App Tracking Transparency permission and Android advertising settings affect some tracking uses. Declining tracking does not necessarily remove ads or all ad-related processing. Non-personalised ads can still use technical data and storage. Ad-consent controls, where presented, are separate from account sign-in, AI disclosure and device notification permission.

Rewarded ads are optional actions tied to a benefit. Access or credit records are stored to apply the benefit and prevent duplicate rewards. Pro changes the ad and credit experience described in the offer; it does not stop essential account, security or purchase processing. A direct sponsor’s external page has its own privacy practices when you open it.

Learn more about how Google uses information from partner services and AdMob privacy choices.

Optional purchase diagnostics

The app currently offers a separate, default-off “Share purchase diagnostics” choice for measuring Pro offer views, purchase attempts and outcomes with Firebase Analytics. Those custom events use fields such as offer entry point, product, outcome, currency and displayed price, not your notes, marks, receipt or name. Analytics services may still associate events with app-instance and device information. Turning this option off does not switch off essential payment verification, service logs or AdMob’s separate processing.

Website and device storage

Device permissions: camera, photo or file access is used when you choose to capture or attach media, scan a question, or open a document. Microphone permission applies to features that record audio or video with sound, not ordinary playback of an audio lesson. Notification permission supports messages, mentions, school updates, study reminders and account notices. Background-audio and alarm capabilities support playback or scheduled reminders; they are not permission to record you in the background.

You can revoke permissions in your device or browser settings. Doing so can stop the related feature while leaving other features available. Push notifications can show message or learning information on a lock screen, depending on your settings; turn off previews on a shared device. We use a push token to address notifications to an installation. An app permission is not consent to unrelated marketing or advertising tracking.

The current Study Surge marketing pages and this policy page do not include advertising pixels or optional analytics scripts. Hosts still receive ordinary network requests and may keep operational logs. The signed-in Simple Study workspace uses Firebase sign-in persistence and browser storage to keep your session; its PDF preview worker is fetched from the UNPKG content-delivery service, which receives a network request, not your PDF through that download.

The mobile app keeps settings, consent choices, downloads and some learning records locally. Clearing browser or app data can sign you out or permanently remove local-only work. It does not itself delete cloud records. On a shared device, signing out may leave downloaded files and locally personalised notes available to the next person using that device.

Purchases, rewards and payouts

Apple and Google handle in-app checkout and send purchase receipts or tokens and status information for entitlement verification. Active and historical subscription records can include the product, store, dates, renewal or cancellation status and reason, and verification outcome. Cancellation normally affects renewal; a paid entitlement may continue until the end of its term.

Yoco handles Simple Study card checkout. Our systems create payment-intent and credit records and receive payment events containing transaction and checkout metadata. Enter card details only on the payment provider's checkout. Full card numbers and card security codes are not required for your learning profile or support requests. Never send a card number, PIN or one-time password through notes, chat or email.

Invite redemptions and creator payouts may require a recipient name, phone number for airtime, or bank name, account-holder name, account number, account type and branch code, depending on the selected payment method. We keep the request, amount, status, relevant access or reward records, dates and payment or rejection notes to process the request, reconcile it and investigate disputes. Authorised staff and the payment or banking service handling the payout may access necessary details. Bank details are not intended to appear in public posts or creator profiles.

For a payout, provide accurate details for an account you are authorised to use. We may review relevant transaction, access and reward records before resolving a claim or dispute. Payout eligibility, rates, minimum amounts and subscription prices are set out in the applicable product offer or terms. Young learners should involve a parent or lawful guardian before providing payment information.

13

How long information is kept

Retention varies by record type and purpose. We consider the account or feature being provided, outstanding transactions or disputes, security needs, and applicable record-keeping duties. The following criteria apply rather than a single deletion period for every record.

Accounts and cloud learning records
Kept to operate your account and saved features, subject to a valid deletion request and any specific lawful retention need. This includes saved Simple Study packs and signed-in readiness or marks records.
Public content, chats and uploaded media
Kept for the relevant sharing or conversation service and, where necessary, a report or dispute. Profile deletion and story expiry do not automatically delete all associated content. The email deletion route below covers associated records as well as the account.
Local notes, mock exams and downloads
Remain on the device until removed through the relevant controls, cleared with app or browser data, or deleted from the download location. Uninstalling may not remove files exported elsewhere or copies in your device backup.
Payments, rewards and banking details
Retained for completing and reconciling a transaction and the applicable accounting, dispute or fraud-prevention period. You can request correction or deletion, subject to a specific lawful need to retain the record.
Logs, backups and service-provider records
Subject to the relevant operational and provider retention settings. Backups may outlast deletion from an active system. Provider-held AI records can have their own retention or legal-preservation periods, as described above.

For a deletion request, we will explain any records that must be retained, why, and the expected handling period. Retention for a legal claim or record-keeping duty does not permit unrelated marketing use. Uninstalling, signing out, cancelling a subscription and deleting an account are different actions.

14

Your choices and deletion requests

You can ask whether we hold your personal information, request access, correct inaccuracies, request deletion or restriction where applicable, object to a use on applicable grounds, withdraw relevant consent, or complain about our handling. Rights and lawful exceptions depend on the law that applies to your situation. If another jurisdiction gives you additional rights, this policy does not take them away.

How to make a request

  1. Email hello@studysurge.co.za with the subject “Privacy request”. Name the product, or say “both products”.
  2. Explain whether you want access, a correction, account deletion, removal of a study pack or post, or another action. Include your account email or handle and relevant item links if you have them.
  3. We may need proportionate verification before disclosing or changing data. If you cannot access your old email, explain that and we will discuss another route. Do not send your password, an ID document or banking details with an initial request.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law, explain any necessary verification, and tell you if a lawful exception affects the request. Where a law permits a fee for a particular request, we will explain it before proceeding. A support request is not an automated deletion confirmation.

Account and associated-data deletion

Grade 12 All Subjects: use Delete Account in the app to remove your sign-in account and primary profile records. Associated learning records, messages and uploads require a separate email deletion request. You can instead email us for both account and associated-data deletion in one request, including if you cannot use the in-app control.

Simple Study: email us to delete your account and associated study packs, or to remove an individual pack. You may request deletion across both products. You do not need to list every stored record; item links are optional and help us locate a particular item.

Subscriptions: manage renewal in the store where you subscribed. Account deletion does not necessarily cancel a store subscription, and cancelling renewal does not automatically delete your data.

You can also change your profile where editing is available, decline optional fields, manage device permissions, turn off optional purchase diagnostics, remove local downloads, and avoid publishing personal material. Contact us when a control you need is missing or does not work.

For parents and guardians: contact hello@studysurge.co.za to discuss consent, review a child's information, request a correction or deletion, or raise a concern. We may verify your identity and authority to protect the child's information before responding. Where a concern involves processing without required permission, we will investigate and take appropriate action, including restriction or deletion where necessary.

Security and incident handling

Our services use authentication, database and storage access rules, and role-based administrative controls. No online service, device or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. This limitation does not remove our responsibility to provide the safeguards required by applicable law.

Use a unique password, keep your device secure and avoid sharing accounts. Contact us promptly if you suspect account misuse, a leaked document or an unauthorised payment. Do not send additional sensitive data to demonstrate a problem unless we arrange a suitable channel.

We will investigate suspected personal-information security compromises, take appropriate containment steps, and notify the Information Regulator and affected people where required by law. Notification timing and any lawful delay depend on the incident and the applicable requirements. See the Regulator’s security-compromise guidance.

For immediate danger, contact emergency services rather than waiting for a support response. Our inbox is not an emergency or round-the-clock safeguarding service.
